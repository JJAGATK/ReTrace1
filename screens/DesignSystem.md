## Brand & Style

This design system infuses the fluid translucency of Apple Liquid Glass with the high-voltage electricity of Midnight Amethyst and Neon Indigo. Engineered for high-speed, community-driven item recovery, the UI balances atmospheric mystery with instantaneous optical clarity. It transforms what is traditionally an anxious, administrative task into an intuitive, polished social experience.

The visual direction centers on:
- **Liquid Luminosity:** Multi-layered lavender-tinged and midnight-slate frosted glass planes suspended over rich, deep violet ambient voids.
- **Electric Precision:** High-chroma Neon Indigo and Violet Radiance micro-gradients guiding attention toward critical user journeys, confirmations, and alerts.
- **Immediate Emotional Relief:** High-confidence status markers (Luminous Cyan and Emerald) paired with urgent alert pulses (Hot Fuchsia) create immediate cognitive orientation at a glance.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture harnesses an electric spectral range calibrated for multi-layered frosted glass surfaces in both bright daylight and obsidian night conditions.

### Light Theme Surfaces & Tints
- **Canvas Root:** `#F8F7FF` (Subtle lavender-bathed off-white).
- **Glass Panel Surface:** `rgba(250, 249, 255, 0.8)` with active backdrop diffusion.
- **Solid Container:** `#FFFFFF` with violet edge caustics.
- **Hairline Specular Borders:** `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)` upper edges, graduating to `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.06)` along shadow boundaries.

### Dark Theme Surfaces & Tints
- **Canvas Root:** `#0B0C16` (Obsidian slate-violet).
- **Liquid Glass Panels:** `rgba(26, 25, 48, 0.75)` with intense frosted diffusion.
- **Specular Edge Reflections:** `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)` highlighting top and left borders, cascading to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` along bottom edges.

### Semantic & Interaction Accents
- **Primary Glow:** `#6366F1` (Electric Indigo) blending smoothly into `#8B5CF6` (Violet Radiance) for primary action states, gradient strokes, and focus anchors.
- **Verified & Trust:** `#06B6D4` (Luminous Cyan) and `#10B981` (Emerald) denoting verified safe returns, item authentication, and confirmed handoffs.
- **Urgent / Lost Alert Badge:** `#F43F5E` (Hot Fuchsia / Coral) applied to active broadcast beacons, missing item pings, and high-urgency reports.

## Typography

Typography is set exclusively in Inter to ensure crisp optical legibility against semi-opaque liquid glass materials and high-contrast ambient glow fields.

- **Display & Headlines:** Formed with tight tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) and semi-bold/bold weights to anchor headlines against luminous backgrounds. Large display titles adapt through dedicated mobile variants (`display-lg-mobile`, `headline-lg-mobile`) to prevent layout strain on compact viewports.
- **Body & Paragraphs:** Neutral tracking and open line heights (`1.45` to `1.6`) ensure long item descriptions, surrender details, and chat conversations remain effortless to digest.
- **Labels & Micro-Indicators:** Set with positive letter spacing (`+0.01em` to `+0.03em`) and medium-to-bold weights, preserving crisp visibility when rendered inside compact status pills and glass badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is driven by a flexible fluid column system tuned for rapid thumb-driven feed consumption on handhelds and multi-pane spatial clarity on larger screens.

### Spatial Rhythms
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column vertical stream centered within a maximum width of 480px. Margins stay tucked at `1rem`, prioritizing edge-to-edge media visibility with floating bottom control bars.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 2-column card mesh paired with a persistent horizontal category filtration rail. Section gutters expand to `1.25rem`.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 3-column feed with fixed left/right glass command panels for real-time campus dispatch, live notifications, and quick-loss intake workflows. Bounded by a maximum container width of `1240px` and `margin-desktop` gutters.

### Internal Spacing Cadence
- Cards, drawers, and modal panels apply `space-md` or `space-lg` internal clearance.
- Interactive clusters, tags, and micro-metric pairings utilize `space-xs` and `space-sm` for precise grouping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through optical diffusion, glass refraction, and tinted ambient lighting rather than generic drop shadows.

### Glassmorphism & Refraction
- **Standard Card Chassis:** `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(170%)` with high-translucency fluid fills.
- **Floating Controls & Docks:** `backdrop-filter: blur(32px) saturate(190%)` for deep, fluid isolation above moving feed content.
- **Hairline Borders:** Continuous `1px` structural outline with specular light-catching headers:
  - *Light Mode:* `border: 1px solid rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`.
  - *Dark Mode:* `border: 1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)` with upper bevel highlight `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.

### Layered Violet Ambient Shadows
- **Level 1 (Feed Cards):** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(11, 12, 22, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Docks & Menus):** `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.18), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(11, 12, 22, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Alert Sheets & Modals):** `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(11, 12, 22, 0.45), 0 0 32px 0 rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The geometric strategy juxtaposes spacious, rounded structural containers with razor-sharp pill controls to evoke soft liquid tactility.

- **Panels & Cards:** Containers, sheets, and modular cards feature `rounded-2xl` (`1.25rem` / `20px`) geometry for an inviting, tactile profile.
- **Buttons & Status Indicators:** Interactive action triggers, status chips, search bars, and category selectors adopt `rounded-full` (pill) forms.
- **Nested Media:** Photographic previews and image galleries nested within cards take `rounded-xl` (`1rem` / `16px`) corners, preserving balanced concentric radii.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) element with a vibrant gradient from Electric Indigo (`#6366F1`) to Violet Radiance (`#8B5CF6`). Finished with an inset upper specular highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`) and an ambient neon glow (`0 8px 20px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`). Active state applies `transform: scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary Liquid Glass Button:** Pill-shaped glass container (`glass-panel-light` or `glass-panel-dark`), bounded by a `1px` specular violet border. Hover brings a 10% opacity increase and a subtle Indigo aura.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Urgent / Lost Alert:** Pill shape filled with translucent Hot Fuchsia (`rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15)`), `1px` border (`#F43F5E`), Hot Fuchsia label text, and a pulsing 6px beacon dot.
- **Verified / Found:** Translucent Emerald or Luminous Cyan pill with crisp matching borders and icon check accents.

### Feed & Item Cards
- **Architecture:** Constructed on a `rounded-2xl` liquid glass panel with a 1px specular violet hairline border.
- **Media Presentation:** Edge-to-edge media with a 4:5 or 1:1 aspect ratio, slightly inset with `space-sm` padding and `rounded-xl` nested curvature.
- **Floating Meta Overlays:** Glass badges floating on the top-left (status) and top-right (time / distance) of the media element.
- **Footer Interaction Bar:** Clear glass row housing quick response triggers: "Claim Item", "Provide Clue", and "Share".

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Pill or `rounded-xl` glass wells (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.05)` light, `rgba(26, 25, 48, 0.5)` dark) framed by a subtle violet border. Focus state introduces an Electric Indigo glow ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #6366F1`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular pill rings with an Electric Indigo gradient fill on check, accompanied by a bright Emerald validation checkmark.

### Floating Dock Navigation
- Pinned bottom bar floating `1.5rem` above the canvas floor.
- Built with `glass-panel-dark` or `glass-panel-light`, rounded-full silhouette, `blur(32px)`, and an Electric Indigo indicator light that smoothly glides beneath the active route.
